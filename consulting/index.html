Case studies

A few examples of our work. We do not name clients.

Telecom and AI

A leading telecom operator

The challenge. The operator wanted AI in its network operations, but its systems run in a secure, air-gapped environment at the edge, where large cloud-hosted models are not an option.

What we did. We worked with its operations engineering leaders to understand those limits, and proposed a fine-tuned, domain-specific small language model built to run within them.

The result. The proposal won the bid.

Restaurants and digital

A leading restaurant chain

The challenge. The chain relied on traditional ways of taking orders and reaching customers, and wanted to grow its sales.

What we did. We reviewed how orders and customer contact worked today, and moved the key steps online and onto digital tools.

The result. The chain saw its sales improve.

Intellectual property

A growing business

The challenge. The business was about to scale a new brand and product without knowing whether its name and ideas were protected.

What we did. We searched existing trademarks to check the name was clear, filed for trademark protection, and set out what else was worth protecting and in what order.

The result. The business launched with its brand protected and a clear IP plan.

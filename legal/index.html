What it can do

  • Explain what a law says in simple words
  • Find the right section for your question
  • Match old IPC and CrPC numbers to the new BNS and BNSS
  • Suggest what to do next

What it cannot do

  • Give legal advice for your exact situation
  • Predict how a case will end
  • Replace a lawyer

Your questions are not saved on this site. To write an answer, your question and the matching sections of the law are sent securely to our AI provider. Please do not type names, phone numbers or other personal details.
